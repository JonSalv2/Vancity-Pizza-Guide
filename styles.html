Pizza Styles Explained

Learn what makes each style unique.

What to Look For in a Pizza Style

  • A thinner crust usually means a lighter slice, while thicker dough feels more filling.
  • Softer pizzas often emphasize fresh ingredients, while crisp pizzas focus more on texture and structure.
  • Some styles are built for quick grab-and-go eating, while others work better as a full sit-down meal.
  • The same pizza can feel traditional, modern, or experimental depending on the toppings and baking method.
