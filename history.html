A Brief History of Pizza

See how it traveled from Italy to North America

The deliciously diverse world of pizza styles grew out of different cities, ovens, ingredients, and ways of eating.

1800s Neapolitan

Naples, Italy

Neapolitan pizza developed in Naples as a wood-fired style with a soft center, airy crust, and simple toppings like tomato, mozzarella, and basil.

Early 1900s New York Style

New York City, USA

New York style grew from Italian roots into a larger pizza with a crisp yet foldable crust, made for fast city eating.

1950s Sicilian / Grandma

New York City, USA

Sicilian and Grandma pies became known for rectangular pan baking, thicker crusts, and a hearty homemade feel.

1940s Chicago Deep Dish

Chicago, USA

Chicago deep dish became famous for its tall crust, layered fillings, and pie-like structure baked in deep pans.

1940s Detroit Style

Detroit, USA

Detroit style is a rectangular pizza with a crisp, airy crust, baked in steel pans and finished with sauce on top.
